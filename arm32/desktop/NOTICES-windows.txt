What the OpenDMTQ player for Windows brings, and the licenses of those parts. None of this covers the game itself.

OpenDMTQ's player and runtime (https://github.com/kanggu12/openDMTQ) are under the MIT License, below.

Built into OpenDMTQ-Player.exe:
- Dynarmic, the ARM translator, with fmt, mcl, robin-map, xbyak, Zycore and Zydis: their licenses are below.
- LLVM's libc++, libc++abi, libunwind and compiler-rt, from the llvm-mingw toolchain: the Apache License 2.0 with LLVM
  Exceptions, below.
- The mingw-w64 runtime and winpthreads: their licenses are below.
- AMD FidelityFX Super Resolution 1 (its EASU and RCAS, ported to OpenGL ES shaders): the MIT License below.
- NVIDIA Image Scaling SDK (its NVScaler, ported to an OpenGL ES shader, and its NIS_Config.h with the scaler's
  coefficients): the MIT License below.
- SMAA (its shader and its area and search textures): the MIT License below.
- Intel's CMAA 2 (its compute shaders, ported to OpenGL ES and changed as their source says): the Apache License 2.0
  below.

Beside it:
- ANGLE (libEGL.dll, libGLESv2.dll), OpenGL ES on Direct3D: the BSD license below.
- zlib (zlib1.dll): the zlib license below.
- The GCC runtime ANGLE's DLLs were built with (libgcc_s_seh-1.dll, libstdc++-6.dll): the GNU GPL 3 with the GCC Runtime
  Library Exception, below.
- winpthreads (libwinpthread-1.dll): its license is below.
- Pretendard (fonts\Pretendard-*.ttf), the face of the overlay's words, unchanged, and DMTQ Wide
  (fonts\DMTQWide-ExtraBold.ttf), the launcher's wordmark face, a cut of Saira: the SIL Open Font License 1.1, below
  and beside them.

In runtime/sysroot: Android's 32-bit linker and system libraries (libc, libm, libdl, libstdc++, libc++, libz, liblog),
from the Android Open Source Project, unchanged, by way of Google's Android 6.0 ARM system image. They are under the
Apache License 2.0 and BSD, MIT, ISC, zlib and NCSA licenses. Their notices are in android-NOTICE.html.gz, next to this
file, which names each of them but libc++; libc++'s license is below. The time zones (runtime/sysroot/usr/share/zoneinfo)
come from the IANA time zone database, which is in the public domain.
